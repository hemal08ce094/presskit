What is new

Version 2.4 adds a Haunted sky tour (six objects with a two-word name, a plain name and tonight's rise time for the phone's location), ghosts in the dark sky with a seven-ghost hunt, Hangar stickers for the 47 aircraft types in Learn to Spot, and Skywatch Station (radar dial, ISS countdown, Moon phase and a split-flap departures board) on every iPhone and iPad.
