Magic Hour Spots Map

For iPhone and iPad · Privacy policy

Magic Hour Spots Map shows where to take a photo and when the light will be right there. Each spot has a shooting point, the bearing to the subject, a tip, and a light timeline for the day with golden hour and blue hour solved from the sun's altitude in the spot's own time zone.

App Store frames, iPhone 17 Pro and iPad Pro 13-inch, 20 November 2026 in Dubai. Click for full size.

What is in it

  • Spots: a feed of curated photo spots near you or in a city you pick, starting with 30 in Dubai, and Today's three for sunrise, golden hour and blue hour.
  • Light: the sun and moon for where you stand or any point you drop on the map.
  • Look: the day's sun path drawn over the camera view at the spot.
  • Plan: saved spots, light alerts, shot cards and Learn the Light cards.

Free and Pro

Free: the spot feed, today's light and weather at every spot, the AR sun path for today, 3 saved spots and 1 light alert. Pro is a yearly subscription that adds the alignment calendar, other dates on the light timeline, unlimited saved spots and alerts, and shoot plans.
