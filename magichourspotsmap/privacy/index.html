Magic Hour Spots Map privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Back to the app page

Summary

Magic Hour Spots Map has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app has no server of its own, and the developer collects no data about you.

Location

  • Your location is used on your device to find photo spots near you and to work out sunrise, golden hour and blue hour where you stand.
  • To fetch a forecast, the app sends coordinates to Apple Weather. To look up places in cities without curated spots, it sends coordinates or a place name to Apple Maps and to Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons. These requests carry no name, account or device identifier.
  • Location is optional. Without it you pick a city and the app works the same way for that city.

Photos

A photo you add to a shot card stays inside the app on your device and is never uploaded. The app saves a share card or a Look capture to your library only when you tap Save, with add-only access, so it cannot read the rest of your library.

Camera, motion and compass

Look reads the camera, motion sensors and compass on the device to draw the sun's path over the real view. Camera frames are not recorded or sent anywhere unless you save a capture.

Calendar

When you tap Add to Calendar, the app writes your shoot plan stops to your calendar. It has write-only access and cannot read your events.

What stays on your device

Saved spots, light alerts, shoot plans, shot cards, badges and settings are stored on your device. Light alerts are scheduled on the device; there is no push server. Deleting the app deletes them.

Weather data

Forecasts come from Apple Weather through WeatherKit. Weather data sources.

Spot data and photo credits

Spot names and positions come from Wikidata (CC0). Spot photos come from Wikimedia Commons; each photo shows its author and license in the app, with a link to its file page. Settings > About > Data and credits lists every source.

Purchases

Pro is a yearly subscription handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Sharing

The developer shares nothing with third parties because nothing is collected.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
