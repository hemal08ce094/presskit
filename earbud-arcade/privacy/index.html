Earbud Arcade privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Back to the press kit

Summary

Earbud Arcade has no account, no ads and no tracking. To learn which games and controllers people use, it sends anonymous usage analytics through PostHog. You can turn this off in Settings with Share anonymous usage. Sound from the microphone and motion from your earbud or device never leave your device.

Anonymous usage analytics

  • What is sent: events such as the app opening, an onboarding page shown, a game started, finished or quit (with the game, score, whether it was a personal best, its length and rounds), the controller picked, an earbud centred, the microphone switched on for tap-to-fire, a setting changed, a Siri shortcut used and the rating prompt shown. Each event also carries the details the analytics library adds on every device: app version, operating system version, device model, screen size, language and time zone setting, Wi-Fi or cellular connection, plus the controller in use and whether the build is a test or App Store build.
  • How it is identified: by a random ID created by the app on your device. There is no account, and the app never sends your name, email, Apple ID, contacts, advertising identifier or precise location. Location from your IP address is switched off.
  • Who receives it: PostHog, our analytics provider, which stores the events in the United States on our behalf. The data is used only to improve the app, never to show ads or to track you across apps and websites, and is not sold.
  • How to stop it: open Settings in the app and turn off Share anonymous usage. Nothing more is sent from that moment. Deleting the app also deletes the random ID.

What stays on your device

  • High scores, settings and calibration are stored in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Settings has a Reset scores button.
  • Motion data from your earbud or device is used to move the crosshair while you play. It stays on the device and is not stored.
  • If you turn on firing by tapping the table, the microphone signal is analysed in memory to detect the tap. The audio is never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Motion, to read head tracking from a connected earbud, and device tilt on iPhone and iPad.
  • Microphone, only for tap detection. You can decline it and fire by touch, click or keyboard instead.
  • Bluetooth on Mac, to connect to the earbud.

Sharing

Only the anonymous usage events above leave your device, and only to PostHog. The App Store privacy label lists Usage Data (Product Interaction and Other Usage Data) as data not linked to you, used for analytics, and no data used to track you.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage events are not linked to a name, account or contact detail.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
