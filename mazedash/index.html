Maze Dash: Watch Arcade

Maze chase for Apple Watch · App Store · updated 17 Oct 2026

What is new

Two angles for the winter break. One turn is three lives, and every finished run lands in the On Wrist list with its date and time, so one watch can be passed around a room. After the holidays, four achievements (First Run, Power Player, Century, Ghost Hunter) are announced on the Game Over card. Version 1.4 is live; 1.5 was rejected and is not described here.
