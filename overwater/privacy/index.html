OverWater privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Back to the press kit

Summary

OverWater has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer collects no data about you.

Location

  • Your location is used on your device to work out which ships are near you, their distance and bearing, and to place labels in the camera view.
  • To fetch traffic, the app sends a search area (a bounding box or a centre point and radius around you) to the ship data feed, Fintraffic digitraffic.fi. The request does not include your name, an account or a device identifier.
  • Nothing about you or your location is stored on a server by the developer.

Camera, motion and compass

The camera, motion sensors and compass are read on the device to show ship labels in line with the real view. Camera frames are not recorded or sent anywhere. Camera and location are optional; without them the app shows a list and a drawn horizon.

Photos

Photo mode saves a picture to your library only when you tap Save. The app asks for add-only access then. It cannot read your photo library.

What stays on your device

Favourites, spotted ships, cards, medals, bingo progress and settings are stored in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Pro is a yearly subscription handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Sharing

The developer shares nothing with third parties because nothing is collected. The App Store privacy label lists location, not linked to you, used for app functionality only.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
